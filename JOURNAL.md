# NEXUS Journal 01: Research & Planning
Before starting the project I spend some time in research . in this research I wanted to make NEXUS not only a simple air purifier , I also want some extraordinary features.
## I planned NEXUS in two separate parts
### 1.The main hub- It includes an ESP32-S3 with touchscreen to show temperature, humidity, CO₂, AQI and fan speed
### 2.The remote - It includes a small OLED display and sensors for temperature, humidity, light level and motion and this is fully separated from the main hub.
